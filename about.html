Our Story

Founded in 2019 on a simple belief that every business deserves design that works as hard as they do. We bring together graphic designers, web developers, and print specialists under one roof, giving you a seamless creative experience from concept to delivery.


Based in Etwatwa, Gauteng, we serve businesses across South Africa and across the borders globally. Our work is tailored to various industries, from startups to established enterprises and everything in between. No project is too small for us.

Our Journey
2019

The Beginning

Inked Studios was founded in Etwatwa with a passion for helping local businesses stand out through logo design and print solutions.

2020 - 2021

Adapting Through Change

As the COVID-19 pandemic reshaped the business landscape, Inked Studios embraced digital transformation, expanding into web development to help brands stay visible, connected, and resilient online.

2023

Building Brands with Purpose

The studio evolved into a full service creative partner, offering brand strategy, visual identity development, messaging under one roof.

Now

Growing Studio

380+ projects delivered. Bigger clients. The same commitment to craft.
